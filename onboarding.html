How Payment Works

Quote, complete, get paid.

We handle the money so you can focus on the wrench. No collecting cash. No chasing customers. No bounced checks.

Step 01

Quote the job

After diagnosing, you give the customer a price. Your number — we don't mark it up.

Step 02

We invoice the customer

A Square invoice is sent on your behalf. The customer pays online — card, Apple Pay, or Google Pay.

Step 03

You do the work

Show up, fix it, get the customer's sign-off when the job is complete.

Step 04

You get paid 80%

Next business day, via Cash App. We hold the first job 48 hours to clear disputes — after that, it's same-next-day.
